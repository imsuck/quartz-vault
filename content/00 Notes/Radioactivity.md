$$A=lambda N$$

- $A$: activity (decays per second)
- $lambda$: [[Decay Constant]]
- $N$: number of undecayed nuclei

Radioactivity is measured in Becquerels ($"Bq" = 1 "s"^(-1)$) or Curie ($1 "Ci" = 3.7 times 10^10 "Bq"$)
