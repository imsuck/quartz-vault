Polyprotic acids, acids with multiple protons ($H^+$),  have different modes of ionization, depending on the environment pH level. Carbonic acid, for example, have the following levels of ionization:
$$
// #import "@preview/chemformula:0.1.2": ch
// #ch("H2CO3 <=>[+OH-][+H+] HCO3- <=>[+OH-][+H+] CO3^2-")
"H"_2"CO"_3 stretch(arrows.rl)^("   "+"OH"^-"   ")_(+"H"^+) "HCO"_3^- stretch(arrows.rl)^("   "+"OH"^-"   ")_(+"H"^+) "CO"_3^(2-)
$$
This can be used to predict products of reactions with acidic salts.
