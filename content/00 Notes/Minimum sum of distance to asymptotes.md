### Case $deg(1)/deg(1)$

$$
y=(3x+8)/(x+2)
$$
$$
S= |x+2|+|y-3| = |x+2|+2/(|x+2|)
$$

$S$ is minimized when $|x+2| = sqrt(2)$ via [[AM-GM]] ($min S = 2sqrt(2)$)
