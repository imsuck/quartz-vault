A common, and versatile inequality.
$$
1/n sum_(i=1)^n x_i = root(n,x_1 x_2 dots.h.c  x_n)
$$
Trivial extension:
$$
1/alpha sum alpha_i x_i = root(alpha, product x_i^(a_i))
$$
Where $alpha=sum alpha_i$.
