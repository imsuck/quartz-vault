Given $f(x)=15/(1+14e^(-0.3x))$, maximize $f'(x)$.

The inflection (mirror) of this graph is when $14e^(-0.3x) = 1$, or more generally, for $f(x)=1/(c+d e^(-k x))$ the inflection is at $d e^(-k x) = c$. This is where the derivative is maximized.
