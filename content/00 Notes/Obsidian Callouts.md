## Collapsible blocks

- **Syntax**: `> [!callout]+ Title` or `> [!callout]- Title`
  The former leaves the block expanded by default. Whereas, the latter keeps the block collapsed.

> [!info]+ Expanded by default
> Content

> [!info]- Collapsed by default
> Content
