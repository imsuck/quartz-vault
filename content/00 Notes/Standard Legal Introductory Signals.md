A system for annotating relationships between linked notes.

- **Support**: _see, see also, accord_
- **Direct authority**: _e.g._ (example), _see generally_
- **Contradiction**: _but see, contra_
- **Comparison**: _cf._
- **String continuation**: _and, also_
