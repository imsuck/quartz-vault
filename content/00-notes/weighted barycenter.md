With barycenter $G$, key identity:

$$
sum m_k norm(M X_k)^2 = (sum m_k) norm(M G)^2 + sum m_k norm(G X_k)^2
$$

Sometimes $M$ is constrained to a plane. In that case, the projection of $G$ down the plane minimizes the sum.
