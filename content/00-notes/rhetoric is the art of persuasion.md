According to Aristotle, rhetoric is the art of persuasion, or more specifically, rhetoric is the act of figuring out which means of persuasion are most effective for a given [[rhetorical situation]]. This means using [[rhetorical devices]], like comparison, or considering careful word choices, like "terrorist" versus "freedom fighter".
