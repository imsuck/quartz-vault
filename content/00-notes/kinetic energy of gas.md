Applies for [[ideal gas]]es.
Relates kinetic energy of gas with [[gas temperature]]

$$
K = 3/2 k_B T
$$

- $k_B$: [[Boltzmann constant]]
  For n mols of molecules:
  $$
  K = 3/2 n R T
  $$
- With $N = n N_A$ and $R = k_B N_A$
