Explicit $y = f(x)$

$$
rho = (1+y'^2)^(3/2)/abs(y'')
$$

Parametric $bold(r) = vec(x(t), y(t))$

$$
rho = (x'^2+y'^2)^(3/2)/abs(x'y''-x''y')
$$

Implicit $F(x, y) = 0$

$$
rho = (F_x^2+F_y^2)^(3/2)/abs(F_(x x) F_y^2 - 2F_(x y) F_x F_y + F_(y y)F_x^2)
$$

Polar $r = r(theta)$

$$
rho = (r^2+r'^2)^(3/2)/abs(r^2+2r'^2-r r'')
$$
