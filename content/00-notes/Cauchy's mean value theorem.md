Cauchy's theorem compares the rate of change of two functions.

## Statement

For $f, g: RR->RR$ continuous on $[a, b]$ and differentiable on $(a, b)$, there exists some $c in (a, b)$ such that

$$
(f'(c))/(g'(c)) = (f(b)-f(a))/(g(b)-g(a))
$$

With $g(x) = x$, we get [[Lagrange's mean value theorem]]
