An idea usually lives in the abstract realm, and [[abstract concepts can vary a lot from person to person]]: "a dog" can mean a golden retriever for some but a chihuahua for other. Additionally, [[specification leaves less room for misunderstanding]]s that may arise from the lack of information. Key takeaway: more often than not, people are being too vague rather than being too specific.
