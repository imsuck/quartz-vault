[[mean]] values sometimes go with a [[margin of error]].

### Calculating the margin of error

The margin of error is the [[standard deviation]]

$$
sigma = sqrt(1/(n-1) (sum x^2 - n overline(x)^2)) = sqrt(1/(n-1) (sum x^2 - (sum x)^2/n))
$$
