Note naming: Title case for titles, everything else is written like it's in the middle of a sentence (and not at the beginning).

Types of notes:

- #fleeting notes: mostly to prevent loss of thoughts, insights, or tasks.
- #reference notes: raw data, mostly for look up
  not to be confused with notes in 04-references
- #literature notes: reiterating a thought of someone else in your own words.
  - "Author - claim - year" (or date if needed)
    Kahneman – _People rely on heuristics under uncertainty_ – 2011
    Smith et al. – _Memory is reconstructive, not reproductive_ – 2003
    WHO – _Air pollution increases cardiovascular risk_ – 2022
  - [[précis]] structured (?)
- #atomic notes: single self-contained ideas linked (agressively) with #literature and other #atomic notes
- #molecular notes: synthesis of more complex ideas
- #moc: notes composed of other notes (mostly #atomic notes), mostly for organization

Additionally use [[standard legal introductory signals]].
