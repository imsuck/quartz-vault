## Statement

Lagrange's MVT states that if $f: RR->RR$ is continuous on $[a, b]$ and differentiable on $(a, b)$, then, for some $c in (a, b)$,

$$
f'(c) = (f(b)-f(a))/(b-a)
$$

and (appropriately)

$$
min_((a, b)) f' <= f'(c) <= max_((a, b)) f'
$$

A more general version of Lagrange's MVT is [[Cauchy's mean value theorem]].
