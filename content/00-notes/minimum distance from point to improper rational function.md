Special case for deg(2/1) [[improper rational function]]:

$$
A=(x, f(x)) \
norm(I A)^2 = (x-x_I)^2 + (f(x) - y_I)^2
$$

Sometimes you can use [[variable substitution]] to get [[sum of linear and reciprocal]] and then apply [[AM-GM inequality]] to solve for optimum.
